I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Iowa State University](https://www.cs.iastate.edu/), advised by Christopher J. Quinn. My research is on **Bayesian optimization**, **Gaussian processes**, and **preference learning** with humans in the loop.

My current work focuses on approximate inference methods for **preferential Gaussian processes**, which learn latent utility functions from human choice feedback, with applications to **preference-based Bayesian optimization**. I am also working on uncertainty quantification–based fine-tuning for **large language models**.

Before Iowa State, I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering from Kwangwoon University in Seoul, Korea, where I worked in the Computer Communications Lab on GAN-based data augmentation for object detection. That work led to a first-author journal paper in *Sensors* and a registered Korean patent.

You can download my CV [here](/files/Hyungtak_Lee_CV.pdf).

**I am looking for research / applied scientist internships for Summer 2027.** If you think I'd be a good fit, please [email me](mailto:hyungtak@hyungtak.com).
